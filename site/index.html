01

A tidy library

Drop AppImages into watched folders or integrate one from anywhere. AppHolder creates desktop launchers and keeps your collection together.

02

Updates you can trust

Use update information published with AppImages, review available updates, and verify downloaded files before they replace your current version.

03

Easy to undo

Keep a configurable number of previous versions, browse version history, and roll back when an update does not work out.

04

Built for your desktop

A Go background service powers the native GTK interface, tray icon, notifications, and optional per-app Bubblewrap sandboxing.
